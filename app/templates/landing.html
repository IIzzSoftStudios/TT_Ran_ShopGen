The Problem

Players always test the economy—buying in bulk, selling loot, asking what’s actually on the shelf. Static shop lists and flat prices fall apart fast, and you’re left making up numbers mid-session.

The Forge

Econo-Forge models shops, stock, and prices as one system. When the party clears a market or a war cuts supply, the world responds—and you don’t have to track every change yourself.

Core Features

Prices that react Costs rise when stock runs low or a region is strained. A sword in the capital shouldn’t price like one hauled through a famine—unless you want it to.
Inventories that stick Shops, stashes, and character gear share the same counts. Sell the last potion in town and it’s gone until restock—not “wait, did we have more?”
GM hub Add items, invite players, and advance the simulation between sessions—all from one place.

Built for long campaigns

This isn’t a loot roller—it’s a campaign economy. Designed with Pathfinder and D&D 5E in mind, for tables where trade and scarcity matter. Whether you run a sandbox or a year-long arc, stock and prices stay consistent so players trust what they see at the shop.
