The Problem

Long campaigns sprawl—spreadsheet shops, separate map files, monster notes, player messages, and improvised answers when someone asks what’s on the shelf or where that city actually is. Prep fragments across tools; the table waits while you patch holes live.

The Forge

Econo-Forge keeps world-building, compendiums, maps, economy, and encounter prep in one GM workspace. You run story at the table; the hub runs commerce between sessions and gives players one place to check maps, shops, gear, and shared encounters.
